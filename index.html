FAQ

Questions agencies ask first.

Will my client know you exist?

No. Reports carry your logo and name, we never contact your client unless you ask us to, and we’ll sign your NDA.

Who owns the ad accounts?

Your client, or you. We work through partner access in Meta Business Manager (and the Google and TikTok equivalents). If we stop working together, you remove our access and everything stays where it is.

What does it cost?

White label starts at €450 per client account a month. The final price depends on the client’s ad spend, the channels and how much creative work is involved. You get a fixed quote after the first call. Ad spend is paid by the client straight to the platform.

Why are you so much cheaper than hiring locally?

We’re based in Riga, Latvia, where salaries and office costs are a fraction of those in Germany, the Netherlands, the UK or the US. The platforms, the tools and the skills are the same. You get a senior team without paying for a senior hire.

Where are you based, and which language do we work in?

Riga, Latvia, in the EU. We work in English. Riga is one hour ahead of Berlin, Amsterdam and Zurich all year round, and two hours ahead of London.

Is it GDPR-compliant?

We’re based in the EU, so GDPR applies to us directly. We’ll sign a data processing agreement with your agency, and client data stays in the ad platforms and tools you approve.

How do we communicate?

However your team already works: Slack, WhatsApp, e-mail or a weekly call. A real person answers, with no ticket system in between.

Can you work with our designers?

Yes. We write the angles, hooks and briefs, your designers produce the ads, and we test what they make. If you don’t have designers for ad work, tell us and we’ll find a setup that works.

How does the referral model work?

You introduce a client who needs paid ads. We sign and serve them directly under our own name, and you get a one-off fee from €450 for each client who signs.

How do we start?

Send the partnership request below. We reply to set up a kickoff call, send a proposal for the first client, and start once you’ve approved it and given us access.
