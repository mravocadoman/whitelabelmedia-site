White label reporting for paid social: what to send clients every month

By Rihards Zeila · 12 November 2026

Clients don't read dashboards. They read one page that says what was spent, what came back, what worked and what happens next. Here is a white-label report layout that does that, under your agency's logo.

What white label reporting means

A white-label report is prepared by a partner and delivered under your agency's brand: your logo, your colours, your contact details. The client sees one consistent agency, whoever ran the campaigns.

Good white-label reporting is also the part of the partnership your client actually sees. The ads can be excellent, but if the report is a raw export from Ads Manager, the client will judge the work by it.

Which numbers to include

Pick the numbers that match the client's goal, and stop there. Four is enough for the headline.

Client goalHeadline numbersLeave out of the headline
Online salesSpend, purchases, cost per purchase, ROASReach, CPM, link clicks
LeadsSpend, leads, cost per lead, qualified leads (from the client's CRM)Clicks, CTR
Bookings or visitsSpend, bookings, cost per booking, new vs returning customersImpressions
AwarenessSpend, reach, frequency, cost per 1,000 people reachedConversions it wasn't optimised for

Vanity metrics aren't useless. They help explain why results moved, so keep them in an appendix or in the call, not on the first page.

Turn numbers into next steps

The last box is the one clients remember. Every report should end with three plain actions, for example:

  • Scale what worked, with the budget change in euros
  • Test one new idea, and say why
  • Stop what didn't work

When results dip, say so first and explain the cause in one sentence: seasonality, stock, a tracking change, a new competitor. Clients forgive a bad month far more easily than a report that hides it.

Tools and delivery

Most agencies use a reporting tool that pulls data from Meta and lets you add your own branding, or a well-designed slide or PDF template. Whatever you use:

  • send it on the same day every month,
  • use the client's own time zone and currency,
  • keep the partner's name off the report, but keep their data honest and unedited.

Questions agencies ask

What should a Facebook ads report include?

Spend, the result the campaign was optimised for, cost per result, and return on ad spend where there are sales, plus the trend, the best ad and next steps.

How often should I send clients a paid social report?

Monthly, with a short call, is standard. Large accounts may also get a short weekly update.

Can a white label partner make reports with my logo?

Yes. That is the point of white-label reporting: the partner prepares it, and it goes to the client under your agency's brand.
