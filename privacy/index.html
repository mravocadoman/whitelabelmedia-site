Privacy notice

This notice explains what we do with the details you send through whitelabelmedia.eu, who receives them and what your rights are.

Who we are

White Label Media (whitelabelmedia.eu) is run by Rihards Zeila, Riga, Latvia, who is the data controller. Contact: rihards@marketingaskola.lv, +371 26 673 384.

What we collect

  • About your agency: agency name, website, country, team size, the services you offer, the partnership model and channels you’re interested in, number of client accounts, typical ad spend, start date, what you bill clients (if you give it), whether you need an NDA, and any notes.
  • Contact details: your name, role, e-mail, phone or WhatsApp number (optional) and how you prefer to be contacted.
  • Calculator figures: if you used the margin calculator before sending the form, the numbers you entered there.
  • Technical context: the page you sent the form from, how you reached us (for example, the campaign tag in a link or the referring website), and the time you agreed to this notice.
  • Analytics and advertising data, only if you accept cookies: see Cookies below.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To reply to your request and prepare a proposal. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To prevent spam and abuse, and to see which channels bring requests. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To measure how the site and our ads perform, and to show our ads to people who visited. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you give in the cookie banner.

Who receives it

  • Service providers that process data for us: n8n (form processing and storage), Google (Gmail, for the e-mail we receive), Telegram (a short alert with your name, agency and contact details) and SiteGround (website hosting).
  • Analytics and advertising providers, only if you accept cookies: see below.
  • We don’t share your form details with anyone else, never sell them and never add you to a mailing list.

The website loads no third-party fonts. Analytics and advertising scripts load only after you accept them.

Cookies and storage on your device

Analytics and advertising cookies stay off until you choose “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you accept, your browser loads:

  • Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd): which pages are visited and which parts are used, such as the calculator or the request form, so we can see what works.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd): page visits, which parts of the page you use and whether a request was sent, to measure our ads and show them to people who visited.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag (LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company): page visits, to measure our ads on LinkedIn and show them to people who visited.

These providers set their own cookies and receive your IP address and browser details. They may process data in the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We never pass them your name, e-mail, phone number or message. When a request is sent, they only learn the partnership model, country and number of accounts you picked. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

While you fill in the request form, your answers are saved in your own browser’s storage so a reload doesn’t lose them, and they’re cleared when you send the form. We also keep a note of how you arrived for the duration of your visit. None of this is shared until you press send.

If you add your logo to the sample report, it stays in your browser tab. It is never uploaded.

How long we keep it

If we don’t start working together, we delete your request 12 months after we receive it. If we do, we keep your details for as long as the partnership lasts and as long as accounting law requires afterwards. We delete them sooner if you ask and the law allows it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy, by e-mailing rihards@marketingaskola.lv. You also have the right to complain to Latvia’s Data State Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv).
